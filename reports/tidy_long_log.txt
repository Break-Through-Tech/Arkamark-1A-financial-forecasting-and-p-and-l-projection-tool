Read pct_of_rows as a reporting rate, not a defect. research_development
is low because most of these companies do no R&D, which is a fact about
them rather than a gap in the data.

To get a wide table for one specific analysis, pivot only what you need:

  read_csv('data/clean/fin_long_annual.csv') |>
    filter(line_item %in% c('total_revenue','gross_profit')) |>
    pivot_wider(names_from = line_item, values_from = value) |>
    drop_na()

That yields a dense table with no filling, because you chose two
columns that companies actually report instead of all 40.
